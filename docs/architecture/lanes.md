# Account lanes

A driver that reads an account's quota windows reports them per step through `ProviderReport.quota` (domain/driver.ts, `reportProviderQuota`), and the loop notes them on the account the step's request went out on (`CredentialPlacement.noteQuota`). Quota state belongs to a sign-in, not to its label: each report, reading and segment carries the stamp of the credential it was for (`AuthInfo.signedInAt`), each lane's accounts carry the stamp the store holds now (`credentialLane`, through `ModelRegistry.stamp`), and placement binds each label to that sign-in. A new sign-in under a label starts with no reading, and a late report of a sign-in its label replaced since is dropped. A rename in /auth (`auth.renameKey`) tells the placement (`renameLabel`): the readings and holds go with the sign-in to its new label, and a late report under the old label counts on the new one. A stamp names a sign-in only under its own label: two labels whose sign-ins share a stamp stay two accounts. The Anthropic driver reads them on a Claude plan sign-in, from every answer, a 429 included (`unifiedQuota`, anthropic.ts): `anthropic-ratelimit-unified-{5h,7d}-utilization` (the fraction used) and `-reset` (epoch seconds), and the account's `-status` and `-representative-claim`; the names are the ones Claude Code 2.1.306 reads, and an answer with no status says nothing. A refused account (`rejected`, its claim) holds that window, full when no utilization is given, until its reset, and the placement holds the account while that reading stands: the hold is read from the readings, so a server that loads them after a restart holds it too. The OpenAI driver reads them on a ChatGPT plan sign-in, from every Codex answer, a 429 included (`codexQuota`, openai.ts): `x-codex-{primary,secondary}-used-percent` (0 to 100), `-window-minutes` (the window's name, `5h` or `7d`, comes from it) and `-reset-at` (epoch seconds), the names Codex reads (openai/codex `342ed69`); a window at 100% is rejected until its reset, as credits past it are extra usage and never quota, and other limit families (`x-<limit>-…`, single models) are not read. An API key gets none. A driver may instead limit a model by what it costs (`ModelDriverContribution.spendLimits`: at most `usd` of list price in any trailing `window`). The OpenCode Go driver sets 20%, 50% and 100% of each model's monthly dollars over `5h`, `7d` and `31d` (`goSpendLimits`, opencode.ts; `OPENCODE_GO_PLAN=plus` reads Go Plus, any other value Go), and none on a free model or on Zen. Gent meters each account's own priced spend on such a model per hour (`noteFuel` with `spendLimitsOf`, a step's and a summary's alike) and reads each limit as that model's window (`meterReadings`): the spend in the trailing window over the limit, rejected at the limit (spend past it is extra usage, never quota) until the oldest hour whose leaving brings it under leaves, and its known spend a floor while an unpriced request falls in it: such a window stays rejected once its known spend reached the limit, and is unread otherwise. A request is priced at what its driver names for it (`requestPricing`, through `requestCost`, provider.ts; a step's, a summary's and `models.decide`'s cost alike), else at the model's list price: the Go driver bills a request past its model's long-context line at that tier's price, and a DeepSeek one double in the plan's peak hours (01:00 to 04:00 and 06:00 to 10:00 UTC, Monday to Friday; `goRequestPricing`). A metered window's fuel per percent is its limit's own (`meterRate`), and under its limit it carries one run at least. A new sign-in under the label starts with no metered spend, and a meter goes with its sign-in on a rename. A server that loads the ledger binds each label to the sign-in of its newest stamped reading or meter, so a meter keeps its sign-in after a restart, and a new sign-in after one still starts with none. A reading may name one model (`QuotaReading.model`): a plan that meters a model apart from the account reports that model's window beside the account's of the same name, and the readings, the segments and the ledger key it apart (`windowKey`). A rejected model window holds the account for that model's runs alone (`meters`, through `isHeld`); its segments count that model's fuel alone (`noteFuel` names the step's resolved model, a summary its own); a start admits against the account's windows and those of the model its route spends on (`LaneRequest.model`; a virtual model's default choice, `routeSpendModel`, turn.ts). `accounts.lanes` lists every window, a model's with its `model`, and counts the account's alone in an account's `sustainable` and the provider's `capacity`; the pool's weekly and five-hour pace reads the account's `7d` and `5h` alone, and a model's window is one of the other window ceilings when a start of that model is admitted. The TUI's binding window still reads every window, a model's among them. Every step and handoff summary on a credential is fuel on that account, pool or not (`noteFuel`): its list price over $5 per million tokens, so one fuel is one input token at that price, with its token classes kept; a price that is unknown, or $0 for tokens spent, is unknown fuel, never zero. Per account window, a segment runs from one reading to the first that moved at least 1% from where it opened, and closes with the fuel spent in it; a reset (its time moved by more than 60 s, or the window fell), a gap over 15 minutes, a refusal or unknown fuel drops it. Fuel per percent is `Σ fuel / Σ points` over the kept segments, each halved per 24 active hours after it; a segment that spent none, or whose move passes its fuel by more than 3 points at the rate of the other kept segments, each fuel at the model weights, is spend outside gent and left out (`outsideShare`); each segment is judged with itself left out, until the kept set holds still; a segment with no other kept segment is kept. `band` is `±0.5 / Σ points` kept. A window with no rate of its own reads the provider's over every account. A plan does not bill each model at its list price, so a segment keeps its fuel by model (`FuelSegment.models`) and its edge steps' fuel (`edges`); `fitModelWeights` fits a quota weight per model over the account windows' kept segments: least squares of each move at its window's weighted rate against `Σ weight × fuel`, each row over its edges' rounding variance and its account's window count, a one-point prior at 1, fitted over a 24-hour span with a standard error under 10%, else 1, clamped to [1/8, 8]; windows and weights refit until still (`fitProviderWindows`). `accounts.lanes` reports them (`models`), and each account window its models' fuel per percent (`LaneWindow.models`). Admission still counts runs alike. A window carries `(1 − u) × 100 × fuelPerPct / (fuel per lane-hour × hours to its reset)` runs (a reset that passed reads full, over the window's length); an account the fewest of its windows (0 while held). These per-account rates still decide under-pace placement and reset-first ranking; they do not divide the pool's rolling forecast among accounts. A provider's `capacity` paces its scope (the pool, else the order's first credential) in fuel/hour. Its weekly floor is the largest cumulative in-hand quota divided by time to each reset deadline. Its rolling rate is the in-hand weekly quota plus each fresh week's share inside the next 168 hours (`full × max(0, 168 − hoursToReset) / 168`), divided by 168 hours. The target is `max(floor, rolling)`, capped by weekly and five-hour ceilings. A weekly prefix ceiling divides all fuel available before each reset by elapsed hours, then credits that refill; simultaneous resets share the same pre-refill bound. The five-hour ceiling includes only accounts with weekly room now, plus a spent week's fresh five-hour room from its reset when that falls within five hours; a reset in six hours adds none. An independently rejected five-hour window still holds until its own reset. Short-window fuel is bounded by weekly room on the same account, so complementary windows cannot create fuel. Every refill is credited after its prefix bound, and refilled short-window fuel must also last five hours. Gent uses plain hours and a 100% quota target: it has no day/night pacing setting. `capacity = floor(min(target, weekly ceiling, five-hour ceiling, other window ceilings) / fuelPerHour)`, with one final floor. Unknown readings or rates keep capacity unknown; an entirely held scope is zero. Explicit credential refusals contribute no forecast; quota rejections contribute their future refill but stay held for present placement. A scope without weekly readings keeps the sum of account minima. Pools with no readings keep stage-0 ranking. A pinned scope cannot borrow another account's refill. Fuel per lane-hour is measured: a lane that ends adds its fuel and its hours to its provider's burn, the burn before it halved per 24 lane-hours, and a lane with unknown fuel, or of a provider gent read no window of, adds nothing; until the provider saw 1 lane-hour it is the prior, 3.4 M. A lane of a model gent reads a window of adds to that model's burn too (`modelBurns`), and a model's window paces its runs at that model's rate (`modelFuelPerHourOf`): its measured burn once its lanes saw 1 lane-hour, else the prior times the model's price over the reference model's (`priceRatioOf`: its metered fuel over the same tokens at the reference weights, input 1, output 5, cache read 0.1, cache write 1.25), else the provider's. `accounts.lanes` reports it (`fuelPerHour`) with the lane-hours behind it (`laneHours`). `accounts.lanes` (rpc.ts, no session: the accounts are the server's, the scope the launch profile's config) answers each provider gent read a window of, with its `load`. A run's lane is the route its session's turns take (`credentialLane`: the pin, else the pool, else the order's first credential). `CredentialPlacement.admitLane` admits a run that another run starts (threads, through `ExtensionContext.Accounts.admit`): the load is the running turns and the admitted starts that share an account with the scope, the asker's own turn left out; the account's windows count that load, and a model's own windows count only its runs and starts of that model (each keeps the model its route spends on); at the capacity of either it refuses with `LaneUnavailable` (`Busy`, retry in 60 s; `Spent` at capacity 0, retry at the soonest reset or hold end), and an unknown capacity admits. A project holds lanes (`providers.<owner>.authHold`, read from the user file alone, keyed by repository path with `~` for home; `ConfigService.laneHolds`): a run's project is the nearest directory at or above its cwd that holds `.git`, named by its `origin` url without quotes, scheme, user and `.git` (a scheme url keeps its port; git's url rewrites are not applied), so a clone, a worktree and a rift of one repository are one project, else by that root's real path, else by the cwd's (`ConfigService.project`; a worktree's `.git` file names its git directory, whose `commondir` holds the config). A run of a project under its hold, counted over that project's turns and starts of any model on every account of the sign-in (the asker's own turn left out, as for the pool), is admitted whatever the pace, and refused only when every account of the scope is held (`Spent`); past its hold, the project's runs take pool lanes as any run does. Every other run counts the lanes other projects hold and do not use beside its load, and its `Busy` message names them. `accounts.lanes` does not report holds. An admitted start holds its lane under its message id until its turn opens (`openLane`, at each turn's start, a resumed turn included), until its caller frees it (`ExtensionContext.Accounts.cancel`, `freeReservation`: a start that opens no turn of its own, as a round that joins its thread's running turn, one the thread refuses as busy, or one whose copy fails), or for 2 minutes as a backstop (a start that holds it through its preparation, `Accounts.hold`, keeps it while any hold's scope is open, and its 2 minutes start again when the last hold on that admission ends); the lane frees at the turn's end, before its `turnAfter` hooks, and on the turn's exit as a backstop. Only starts are refused: a user's own turn is counted, never refused. The ledger also keeps the latest readings and the segments, 7 days and 500 per window at most, the burn, the model burns, and the meters until their hours leave the longest window, as optional `readings`, `segments`, `burn`, `modelBurn` and `meters` keys, written whenever there are any: an older file decodes with none, and an older binary ignores them. A value the window drops changes the ledger, so the next write leaves it off the file. A SIGKILL loses what changed since the last write (each hour as it closes, and shutdown); the running turns are in memory alone, so a server after a restart counts no lane until a cut turn runs again.
